Programs & Fees

Three ways to train, one structured program behind all of them. Every fee on this page is the full price — nothing is added later.

01

Group Fitness Batch

Small-group training capped at six people, so nobody trains unwatched. A complete, structured program with proper progression — not a single-format class. You can train from home or gym.

  • Only 4–6 members per batch — no crowd, real personal attention
  • 50-minute sessions
  • 3 days a week or 5 days a week
  • All five formats — HIIT, flexibility, core, Mat Pilates, cardio & functional
  • Seats are limited and close once the batch is full
