About Your Trainer

Twenty Years On The Floor

I am Swapnil Abhyankar — a fitness coach with more than twenty years of experience, having trained hundreds of clients in that time. I am now opening a small-group training batch, and this page is where the details live.

The batch is capped at four to six people on purpose. Six is about the most anyone can genuinely watch in a fifty-minute session, and being watched is the entire point of training with a coach rather than following a video.

What you get is a complete, structured program with proper progression — HIIT, flexibility, core, Mat Pilates, and cardio and functional work. Not a single-format class repeated until you are bored of it.

How I Coach

Three Things I Hold To

These are the reasons the batch is small, the sessions are structured, and the format never stays the same for long.

01

Small Numbers

Four to six people, never more. A crowded floor means someone is training unwatched, and the person training unwatched is the one who gets hurt or gets nowhere.

02

Structure, Not Sessions

A program with proper progression, not a single format on repeat. Five disciplines, sequenced so each one supports the next rather than competing with it.

03

Wherever You Are

Home or gym, it does not matter. The program is written to what you actually have access to, because the plan you can follow beats the plan that needs a facility you do not have.

Before You Ask

Questions I Get Weekly

If yours is not here, send it on WhatsApp. I answer them myself.

How many people are in a batch?

Four to six, never more. That cap is the whole point — six is about the most one coach can genuinely watch across a fifty-minute session. A bigger batch would be cheaper to run and worse to train in.

How long is a session, and how often?

Fifty minutes. You can train three days a week or five days a week, and the fee differs accordingly — see the fees table.

What does a session actually include?

Across the week you cover all five formats: HIIT, flexibility training, core training, Mat Pilates, and cardio and functional training. It is a complete, structured program with proper progression — not a single-format class repeated.

Can I train from home?

Yes. You can exercise from anywhere — home or gym. The programming is written to the equipment you actually have.

Is the quarterly plan cheaper?

Yes, noticeably. Three months at three days a week is ₹10,500, which works out to about ₹3,500 a month against ₹4,000 monthly. Five days a week is ₹17,000 for three months, about ₹5,667 a month against ₹6,500.

What is 2-person training?

Any two people training together — a couple, friends, siblings. You get close to the attention of one-on-one coaching at a lower cost each: ₹6,000 per person per month for three days a week, or ₹10,000 for five.

I have never trained before. Is that a problem?

No. Movements are scaled per person inside the same session, so a beginner and someone experienced can train in the same batch without either being held back or thrown in too deep.
